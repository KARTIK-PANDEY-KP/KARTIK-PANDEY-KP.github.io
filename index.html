03 — Research

Explanations you can check, from a model you can run offline

NAACL 2025 · Long paper

CAVE: Controllable Authorship Verification Explanations

Sahana Ramnath, Kartik Pandey, Elizabeth Boschee, Xiang Ren

Authorship verification asks whether two documents share an author. It is often used in privacy-sensitive settings where a model has to run on premises. CAVE is a fine-tuned Llama-3-8B model that produces free-text explanations with a uniform structure, grounded in linguistic features, that can be checked for consistency with the predicted label.

Proceedings of the 2025 Conference of the Nations of the Americas Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics: Human Language Technologies, Volume 1, pages 8939–8961.

ACL Anthology ↗ PDF ↗ USC ISI listing ↗
  1. 1

    Prompt-CAVE

    A prompt-based method generates silver-standard training explanations grounded in linguistic features.

  2. 2

    Cons-R-L filter

    A consistency metric keeps only examples whose rationale agrees with the label.

  3. 3

    Offline fine-tune

    Llama-3-8B is fine-tuned on the filtered data and evaluated on three authorship verification datasets.
